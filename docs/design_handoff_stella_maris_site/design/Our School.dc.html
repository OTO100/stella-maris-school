Welcome

Principal's message

CC Catherine Cyprian Principal principal@stellamaris.school.nz

Dear Parent or Caregiver — thank you for your interest in Stella Maris Primary School. I welcome your inquiry and consideration of our school community as a place for your child's education.

Stella Maris has a strong academic focus and each child is encouraged to take responsibility for their learning, valuing knowledge, truth and faith, under the guidance of dedicated teachers and support staff. At Stella Maris we value the development of the whole person, integrating spiritual development, academic success, cultural prowess and awareness, and a high level of sporting participation and skill in the context of our Catholic tradition.

The Stella Maris community is based on family. This extended family consists of students, teachers, parents, and extended family joining together to form a community that is wholeheartedly committed to the education of our young people.

As a Catholic school, all we undertake is underpinned by Gospel and Marian Values. Our school is founded on the Marian Values and these values are explicit within our school environment. There is an expectation that the actions and behaviour of all in our school community are guided by these values.

We encourage and indeed expect that our students will become lifelong learners, each developing the Spirit of Stella Maris to take with them out into the workplace and adult life. You will find the Spirit of Stella Maris evident in our students and staff, our approach to learning, friendships, and achievement. Confidence, persistence, resilience, self-reliance are all guiding foundations for carving out a successful pathway through life. These attributes are encapsulated in Learner Capabilities and are reflected in the teaching and learning programme at Stella Maris.

Central to the mission of Stella Maris School is the promotion of excellence. Our commitment to our parents is to support, encourage, nurture, and challenge individual development for all our students and to provide a learning environment which meets their present and future needs.

I look forward to welcoming you and your family into the Stella Maris community.

God Bless — Mā te Atua koe e manaaki

Catherine Cyprian
Principal

Special character

Our name, our faith

Stella Maris, Star of the Sea, is one of the most ancient titles for Our Lady. The name was chosen to honour her and acknowledge her as our protector in Aotearoa, this land surrounded by the sea.

In using this title, we see Our Lady as our guide or leader, someone who could navigate the stars, who watches over us and leads us to Jesus. In following Jesus' way and living the Marian Values we shine for God.

We follow the Marian Values that are deeply rooted in the Gospels. These values are demonstrated by Mary, our Mother and guide. They lead us to a stronger relationship with Her Son, Jesus.

Our Religious Education curriculum is Tō Tātou Whakapono — Our Faith, finalised in 2021, covering Catholic education in Aotearoa New Zealand from Year 1 through to Year 13. It provides guidance for rich learning through four themes: God, The Good News, Our Story and Being Church. It carefully weaves together and scaffolds the heart of the Catholic faith so that this knowledge may connect with the lives of our students and whānau.

Religious Education in Catholic schools “provides a foundation of knowledge which works alongside the whole school's Catholic Special Character, which helps form their understanding of what it means to be a disciple of Jesus and a member of the Catholic Church.” Tō Tātou Whakapono — Our Faith, 2021

Ngā uara — our values

The Marian Values

There is an expectation that the actions and behaviour of everyone in our school community is guided by these six values.

  • Whakapono

    Faith

    “For we live by faith, not by sight.” 2 Corinthians 5:7

    At Stella Maris the Marian value of faith lies at the heart of our community. Inspired by Mary's unwavering trust in God, we foster an environment where we are encouraged to develop a personal relationship with Christ, guided by Mary's example of humility, devotion and obedience to God's will. We nurture the growth of each other's faith, instilling a sense of hope, trust and reverence for Jesus Christ.

    We ask Our Lady, Most Faithful, to pray for us.

  • Aroha

    Love

    “Let us love one another, for love is God.” 1 John 4:7

    Inspired by the compassionate heart of Mary, we foster a safe and nurturing environment where love is celebrated, practised and instilled in the hearts of our students, staff and families. Through our interactions we model the unconditional love that Mary embodies. We strive to create a school community that radiates love, compassion and empathy, where every person is valued, respected and cared for.

    We ask Our Lady, Compassionate Mother, to pray for us.

  • Whakaaronui

    Wisdom

    “The one who gets wisdom loves life; the one who cherishes understanding will soon prosper.” Proverbs 19:8

    Inspired by our Catholic faith and by Mary, the epitome of wisdom, we strive to instil a deep reverence for learning, a hunger for knowledge, and a lifelong pursuit of truth. We challenge and equip each other with critical thinking skills, intellectual curiosity, and the ability to think independently. Our commitment to the Marian Value of wisdom is a guiding light as we build a solid foundation of knowledge, faith and wisdom.

    We ask Our Lady, Seat of Wisdom, to pray for us.

  • Whanaungatanga

    Belonging

    “In Christ we form one body and every member belongs to all the others.” Romans 12:5

    By embracing the value of belonging, we aim to create a school environment that mirrors the love and acceptance of Mary. We celebrate diversity, recognising that each person brings unique gifts and experiences to our school community. Through collaboration and teamwork, we strive to build strong relationships, fostering a genuine sense of belonging and a spirit of unity among all.

    We ask Our Lady, Mother of the Church, to pray for us.

  • Kaitiakitanga

    Guardianship

    “The Lord God took the man into the garden of Eden to cultivate it and keep it.” Genesis 2:15

    Inspired by the example of Mary, who cared for and protected Jesus, we believe that as guardians of God's creation it is our duty to safeguard and preserve the environment, all living creatures, and the resources entrusted to us. We aim to nurture a lifelong commitment to environmental stewardship, ensuring a sustainable future for generations to come.

    We ask Mary, Mother of our Creator, to pray for us.

  • Āwhina

    Service

    “Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.” Matthew 25:40

    We draw inspiration from the life and teachings of Mary, whose unwavering dedication to service continues to guide us. We believe that service is not just an action but a way of life. Through acts of service we instil the importance of reaching out to others, embracing their needs, and uplifting those in need of support.

    We ask Our Lady, Mirror of Justice, to pray for us.
